About the Cooperative

Morong Retailers and Community Multipurpose Cooperative

MORECO is a registered multipurpose cooperative serving the community of Morong, Rizal. Founded to empower local retailers and residents, the cooperative operates on the principle that members are both the owners and the beneficiaries of its services.

Unlike private financial institutions, MORECO channels earnings back to its members through dividends, patronage refunds, and expanded services. Every peso deposited helps another member access a loan, and every loan repaid grows the cooperative's capacity to serve more.

🌐

A Unique Financial Ecosystem

MORECO is built on member ownership — profits flow back to the people who power it, not outside shareholders.

🤝

Community-First Safety Net

Savings, loans, insurance, and livelihood programs give members and their families support at every life stage.
