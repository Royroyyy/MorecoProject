ROLES
=============================================
applicant     Default role after signup. Can apply for membership.
member        Approved member. Can register for events, apply for loans/withdrawals.
admin         Full system access. Manages all content and users.
clerk         Handles QR scanning and fund release.
loan_officer  Reviews and approves loan applications.
