🏛️ About MORECO

A Cooperative Built for the Community

MORECO is a multi-purpose cooperative in Morong, Rizal where members are both the owners and the customers. Instead of focusing purely on profit, MORECO focuses on helping members grow financially through shared resources and collective support.

Members contribute through savings and share capital, and the cooperative uses those funds to provide loans, run programs, and generate dividends shared back to all members.

💰 Financial Services Regular loans, emergency loans, and savings programs at fair rates
🛡️ Member Protection Burial assistance and insurance programs for active members
🛠️ Livelihood Support Business capital and training programs to grow your livelihood
🎓 Education Programs Financial literacy seminars and cooperative education for all members
