Withdrawal Request

Request a withdrawal from your MORECO savings account

How Withdrawals Work
1. Submit your withdrawal request below.
2. A clerk or admin will review and approve it (usually within 1 business day).
3. Once approved, you will receive a QR code in this page and via notification.
4. Visit the MORECO office and present the QR code to a clerk to collect your funds.
5. QR codes are valid for 3 days from approval.
